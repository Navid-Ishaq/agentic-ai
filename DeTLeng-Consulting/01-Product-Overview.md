# DeTLeng Data Readiness Assessment™

> **The Entry Point to Every Successful Data Consulting Engagement**

---

# Executive Summary

The **DeTLeng Data Readiness Assessment™** is not a technical audit.

It is a **business-focused consulting engagement** designed to help organizations understand the current state of their data ecosystem before investing in analytics, cloud migration, reporting automation, AI, or business intelligence initiatives.

Rather than immediately proposing technology, DeTLeng first develops a complete understanding of the client's business, data landscape, reporting processes, operational challenges, and long-term objectives.

This assessment creates the foundation for every future implementation project.

---

# Why This Product Exists

Most consulting companies fail because they immediately begin discussing technology.

Professional consulting firms work differently.

They diagnose first.

Then they prescribe.

Technology comes last.

Exactly like a doctor.

A doctor never performs surgery before diagnosis.

Likewise, DeTLeng never recommends architecture before understanding the business.
