# Purpose

This document defines the development strategy for **Product 04 – Executive Dashboard** within the DeTLeng Consulting Playbook.

The objective is **not** to create a simple Markdown document or technical tutorial.

Instead, the goal is to build a complete **enterprise consulting product** that can be delivered consistently across multiple client engagements.

Every deliverable should follow the same consulting methodology and quality standards used by leading global consulting firms.

---

# Why This Product Matters

Executive Dashboards are often perceived as visualization projects.

In reality, dashboard implementation represents the final layer of a much larger consulting engagement.

Before a dashboard can provide business value, an organization must already possess:

- Reliable business data
- Standardized KPIs
- Automated reporting pipelines
- Validated business metrics
- Secure access controls
- Well-defined governance

Therefore, Product 04 focuses on transforming trusted business data into executive decision-making tools.

---

# Development Philosophy

This product will not be written as:

- Training notes
- AWS tutorials
- Dashboard examples

Instead, it will be developed as a professional consulting asset.

Every section should be reusable, version-controlled, and suitable for real client delivery.
