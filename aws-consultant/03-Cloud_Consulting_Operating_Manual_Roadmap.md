## Agla Mission

Is blueprint ke baad hum ek **book-level Operating Manual** banayenge.

Ye sirf notes ya course nahi hoga.

Ye ek **real-world consulting playbook** hoga jo AWS Solutions Architecture ko SME business consulting ke perspective se sikhayega.

---

# Objective

**50 Real SME Consulting Projects**

Har project ek real business scenario hoga.

Har scenario mein hum client ki problem ko identify karenge, architecture design karenge, implementation plan banayenge aur project ko us level tak le jayenge jahan usay real client ko deliver kiya ja sake.

---

# Standard Project Framework

Har project isi professional structure ko follow karega.

1. 👤 Client Profile
2. 🏢 Business Overview
3. 💥 Business Problem
4. ❓ Discovery Questions (Consultant kya poochega)
5. 🎯 Business Goals
6. ☁️ AWS Architecture
7. 🏗️ Step-by-Step Implementation
8. 💰 AWS Cost Estimation
9. 🔒 Security & Compliance
10. 📄 Professional Client Proposal
11. 📋 Statement of Work (SOW)
12. 📅 Project Timeline
13. 💶 Invoice
14. 🛠️ Support & Maintenance Plan
15. 📈 Future Upsell Opportunities

# End Goal

Ye koi traditional course nahi hoga.

Ye **Accenture + Deloitte + AWS Professional Services** style ka **Consulting Simulator** hoga.

Hum AWS services ko ratenge nahi.

Hum real business problems solve karenge.

Har project ke end par hum ye bhi complete karenge:

- Client Proposal kaise likhni hai.
- Discovery Meeting kaise conduct karni hai.
- Scope kaise define karna hai.
- Price kaise quote karni hai.
- Negotiation kaise karni hai.
- Invoice kaise raise karna hai.
- Support contract kaise banana hai.
- Upsell opportunities kaise identify karni hain.
